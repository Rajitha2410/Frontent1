Google

About the Company

Google is one of the world's leading technology companies founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin. The company provides internet-based services such as Google Search, Gmail, Google Maps, YouTube, Android, Google Cloud, and artificial intelligence technologies. Google helps billions of users around the world access information and digital services. The company focuses on innovation, research, and developing advanced technologies. Google's Bangalore office is one of its important development centers in India, supporting software engineering, cloud computing, and product development.
