Preamble of the Constitution

The Preamble is the introduction to the Constitution of India. It expresses the ideals and objectives of the Constitution and reflects the vision of the people of India.

The Preamble

"WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic and to secure to all its citizens Justice, Liberty, Equality and Fraternity."

Key Values of the Preamble

  • Justice – Social, Economic and Political.
  • Liberty – Freedom of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship.
  • Equality – Equal status and opportunity for every citizen.
  • Fraternity – Brotherhood, unity and integrity of the nation.

Importance of the Preamble

  1. Introduces the Constitution.
  2. Explains the goals of the Constitution.
  3. Reflects the values of Indian democracy.
  4. Guides the Government while making laws.
  5. Promotes unity and equality among citizens.
