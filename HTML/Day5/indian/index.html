Welcome to the Constitution of India

The Constitution of India is the supreme law of our country. It provides the framework for the political system, government, and administration of India. It also defines the rights, duties, and responsibilities of every citizen.

The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into force on 26 January 1950, which is celebrated every year as Republic Day.

Why is the Constitution Important?

  • It is the supreme law of India.
  • It protects the Fundamental Rights of citizens.
  • It explains the powers of the Central and State Governments.
  • It promotes Justice, Liberty, Equality and Fraternity.
  • It ensures democracy and the rule of law.

Key Facts

The Constitution of India is one of the longest written constitutions in the world. It was prepared by the Constituent Assembly under the leadership of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar, who is known as the Father of the Indian Constitution.
