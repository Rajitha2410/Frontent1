Fundamental Duties

Fundamental Duties are the responsibilities that every Indian citizen should follow. They are mentioned in Article 51A of the Indian Constitution. These duties help citizens contribute towards the progress and unity of the nation.

List of Fundamental Duties

  • Respect the Constitution, National Flag and National Anthem.
  • Protect the sovereignty, unity and integrity of India.
  • Defend the country and provide national service when required.
  • Promote harmony and brotherhood among all citizens.
  • Preserve India's rich cultural heritage.
  • Protect the natural environment including forests, rivers and wildlife.
  • Develop scientific thinking and human values.
  • Protect public property and avoid violence.
  • Strive for excellence in all fields.
  • Provide education opportunities for children between 6 and 14 years.

Importance of Fundamental Duties

  1. Creates responsible citizens.
  2. Helps maintain national unity.
  3. Protects culture and environment.
  4. Supports the development of the country.
  5. Encourages respect towards the nation.

Rights and Duties Relationship

Rights and duties are closely connected. While Fundamental Rights provide freedom and protection to citizens, Fundamental Duties remind citizens about their responsibilities towards the nation.

Conclusion

Fundamental Duties guide citizens to become responsible members of society and help in building a strong, peaceful and developed India.
