Fundamental Rights

Fundamental Rights are the basic rights guaranteed to every citizen of India by the Constitution. These rights protect the freedom, dignity, and equality of all people.

Six Fundamental Rights

  1. Right to Equality
    Ensures equal treatment before the law and prohibits discrimination.
  2. Right to Freedom
    Gives freedom of speech, expression, movement, residence, and profession.
  3. Right Against Exploitation
    Prohibits human trafficking, forced labour, and child labour.
  4. Right to Freedom of Religion
    Every citizen is free to follow, practice, and promote any religion.
  5. Cultural and Educational Rights
    Protects the language, culture, and educational rights of minorities.
  6. Right to Constitutional Remedies
    Citizens can approach the courts if their Fundamental Rights are violated.

Importance of Fundamental Rights

  • Protect the dignity of every citizen.
  • Promote equality and justice.
  • Ensure freedom and democracy.
  • Prevent discrimination.
  • Protect people from unfair treatment.

Conclusion

Fundamental Rights are essential for maintaining democracy and ensuring that every citizen lives with freedom, equality, and dignity.
