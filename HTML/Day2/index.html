The Girl Who Was Raised Like a Queen There was a small family living in a beautiful village. The family had a mother, a father, two elder brothers, and one younger daughter. When the girl was very young, her father went abroad for work to support the family. Because of that, she did not get a chance to grow up with her father by her side. But he always loved her very much. He called her often, bought gifts for her, and always wanted to see her happy. Her mother raised her like a queen. She gave her love, care, and support every day. She never let her feel that anything was missing in her life. Her two brothers loved her more than anything. They protected her, cared for her, and always stood by her. They treated her like their own daughter. Whatever she needed, they were always ready to help. She never felt alone because her brothers were always with her. The girl grew up in a village, but her dreams were big. Her family supported everything she wanted. They encouraged her to study well and helped her achieve her goals. After completing college, she started thinking about her future. One day, her mother said, "I want you to build your career in the IT field. I believe you can do it." Those words gave the girl a new hope and confidence. To make her mother's dream come true, she came to Chennai to learn the skills needed for an IT job. Her two brothers were already working in Chennai. They supported her in every step. Whenever she felt worried or afraid, they encouraged her and said, "We are always with you. Believe in yourself." Her mother called her every day to ask if she had eaten and how her studies were going. Her father, who was still working abroad, always said, "My daughter will achieve something great one day." The love, care, and support from her family became her biggest strength. Now, she is not working hard only for herself. She is working hard to make her mother's dream come true, to make her father proud, and to keep the trust of her two loving brothers. She believes one thing with all her heart: **"One day, I will get an IT job. Not only for myself, but for the family who raised me with love and believed in me."**
